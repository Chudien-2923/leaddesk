# Pricing and consultation requirements

Prices are in Vietnamese dong (VND) per month. The management fee is separate from media spend paid to the advertising platform. The published structured pricing policy is the authority for this release; publishing regenerates this pricing document from that policy.

Meta Ads / Facebook Ads management: management fee VND 5,000,000; minimum media spend VND 10,000,000; minimum combined monthly budget VND 15,000,000. A website is not required.

Google Ads management: management fee VND 6,000,000; minimum media spend VND 15,000,000; minimum combined monthly budget VND 21,000,000. A website is required.

A media-only budget does not imply acceptance of the management fee. A combined budget must cover both the fee and the minimum media spend. If the budget basis is unclear, we ask rather than assume. An initial assessment also needs the business, service, website where required and a planned start within 90 days. No assessment guarantees a contract or advertising results.
