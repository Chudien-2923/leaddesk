# Consultation and service policies

## Pricing and assessment

Use only the prices, website requirements and assessment criteria in the active published release. Drafts, preparing or failed releases and internal notes are not customer sources. If service information cannot be retrieved, we do not claim that pricing has been verified.

Assessment needs the business, service, budget basis, website where required and intended start date within 90 days. A total budget must cover the management fee and media spend. A media-only budget requires separate acceptance of the management fee. Missing or conflicting information requires clarification, not an assumption of eligibility.

## Contact details and human review

We save contact details to the agency CRM or save a local request for team review only after the visitor gives the relevant explicit consent. Asking about prices is not consent. A request saved for review is not live chat, notification delivery or a promise that someone has read it. Outside-scope services and specialist or policy concerns require team review rather than automatic acceptance. No response-time, refund, discount or contract terms are promised unless supported by published sources.

## Appointments

Consultations last 30 minutes, Monday to Friday, 09:00–12:00 and 13:00–17:00 in Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh (GMT+7). Times are on a 30-minute grid with at least 24 hours’ notice and a 14-day horizon. The current structured policy is authoritative. Availability depends on the agency calendar; a read failure is not treated as free time.

Both the CRM contact and consultation note must be confirmed saved before times are offered. Selecting a time does not create an appointment. Only the visitor’s final confirmation can request booking after a fresh availability check. We confirm an appointment only after a successful calendar result. An uncertain result requires checking the same booking reference, never creating another booking to escape uncertainty. This page does not send emails, calendar invitations or meeting links.
