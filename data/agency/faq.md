# Frequently asked questions

## Does the management fee include ad spend?

No. The management fee is paid to the agency; media spend is paid separately to the advertising platform. The pricing and consultation requirements in the current published release show both amounts. A combined budget must cover both; it cannot all be treated as media spend.

## Do I need a website for Google Ads or Facebook Ads?

Website requirements are service-specific. Read the current pricing and consultation requirements in this same published release for the Meta Ads / Facebook Ads and Google Ads website conditions. If your chosen service requires a website and you do not have one, that requirement is not met. Do not assume a requirement for one service applies to the other. Website development is not part of advertising management.

## Do you guarantee ROAS, revenue or sales?

No. We do not guarantee ROAS, revenue, order volume or conversion rates. Results depend on the product, market, advertising assets and other factors. An initial assessment is not a promise of results.

## Do you provide SEO, TikTok Ads, website design or legal advice?

Those are outside the published service scope. We do not invent a quote or provide specialist advice beyond Meta Ads and Google Ads. You can request a review by the team after giving contact details and explicit consent.

## What if the published information does not answer my question?

We will say the available sources do not cover it rather than invent policies, fees, source links or results. You may clarify the question or request a review by the team. A review request does not book an appointment or promise a response time.
