# Advertising management services

## Meta Ads and Facebook Ads management

We manage advertising on Meta, including Facebook and Instagram: campaign planning, setup, monitoring and optimization within the agreed scope. The management fee and minimum media spend are separate. See the pricing and consultation requirements in the same published release for the current amounts and website conditions. Media spend is paid to the advertising platform, not included in our management fee.

## Google Ads management

We plan, set up, monitor and optimize Google Ads campaigns. The website requirement, management fee and minimum media spend follow the pricing and consultation requirements in this release. Website development, SEO, branding and full-service content production are not included in advertising management.

## Scope and initial assessment

An initial assessment covers Meta Ads and Google Ads only. Other services require a review by the team; they are not automatically accepted. We ask about your business, preferred service, monthly budget and whether it includes the management fee, website where required, and intended start date. We do not guarantee revenue, orders, conversion rates or ROAS.
